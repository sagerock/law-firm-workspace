# Draft review

1. Identify the document and the matter it belongs to. If it's in `inbox/`, ask which
   matter before reading other files.
2. Read the document, `matter.md`, and `firm/positions.md`.
3. Check:
   - **Facts:** names, relationships, dates, and assets match `matter.md`.
   - **Positions:** anything that departs from `firm/positions.md`.
   - **Consistency:** defined terms, cross-references, internal contradictions.
   - **Gaps:** provisions a careful attorney in this area would expect and doesn't see.
   - **Client goals:** does the document do what **Goals** says the client wants?
4. Report in three groups: **Must fix**, **Should consider**, **Minor**. Each item:
   where (section or quote), the issue, and a suggested fix. Most important first.
5. Mark anything that depends on current law or local practice `[verify]`.
6. Don't edit the original. If the attorney wants a revised version, save it in
   `drafts/` as a new file.
7. Offer to file the document from `inbox/` into the matter's `notes/` or `drafts/`.
