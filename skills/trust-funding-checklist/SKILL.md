# Trust funding checklist

1. From `matter.md` and `notes/`, list every asset the client has mentioned. Ask about
   categories that aren't covered: real estate, bank accounts, brokerage accounts,
   retirement accounts, life insurance, annuities, vehicles, business interests,
   out-of-state property, and tangible personal property.
2. For each asset, write one row:

   | Asset | How it's held now | Action | Who | Status |
   |---|---|---|---|---|

   Typical actions, adjusted to firm positions and jurisdiction:
   - Real estate: new deed to the trustee, recorded in the right county `[verify]`.
   - Bank and brokerage: retitle to the trust, or add a payable/transfer-on-death
     designation per firm positions.
   - Retirement accounts: generally **not** retitled; review beneficiary designations.
   - Life insurance and annuities: review beneficiary designations.
   - Out-of-state real estate: flag for separate handling `[verify]`.
3. Anything unclear gets a question for the client, not a guess.
4. Add each firm-owned step to **Open items** in `matter.md`.
5. Offer to turn the client-owned steps into a plain-English letter with
   `skills/client-letter`.

State-specific rules on deeds, transfer taxes, and designations vary. Mark those
`[verify]` unless the firm's positions already cover them.
