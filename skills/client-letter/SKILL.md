# Client letter

1. Confirm the matter, the recipient, and the one thing this letter must accomplish.
2. Read `firm/voice.md` and the matter's `matter.md` and recent `notes/`.
3. Draft:
   - First line: `DRAFT — attorney review required`.
   - Open with the answer or the purpose, not pleasantries.
   - Plain English. Explain any legal term the first time it appears.
   - Steps or requests as a numbered list with who does what.
   - One clear next step for the client.
   - Sign-off from `firm/voice.md`.
4. Don't promise outcomes, timing, or fees unless the attorney told you to. Follow the
   **Never** list in `firm/voice.md`.
5. Save to `drafts/` as `YYYY-MM-DD client letter - topic.md`.
6. Below the draft, in chat only, list anything you assumed or couldn't confirm.
